\par\section*{Abstract}
This report documents two stages of work. First, the second-round human review instructions (an 808-row register with 251 verbatim reviewer comments) were re-applied row by row to the 798 baseline L4 cards, producing a 777-card recovery release. Second, an independent audit pipeline (AC-01 through AC-08) was applied to that release. Every judgemental step was performed by two independent reviewer agents with consensus-only application, covering restoration of hard-rule violations, absorption of semantic duplicates, MECE curation of every L3 category (duplication and attribution strength), decomposition of composite cards, and literature-grounded reinforcement of under-populated categories. No EM or Hybrid EM was re-run at any stage. \textbf{The final release contains 629 L4 cards: General 487, Agentic 65, Physical 77. All 46 L3 categories hold at least three cards and no Others assignments remain.}
\par\section{Recovery principles}
Valid edits and user-approved merges from the baseline commit were preserved. Only the deletion, merge, split, reassignment, and definition-revision instructions of the second-round review were applied, traced through source\_row\_id lineage. No L3 was created or modified; the L3 master SHA-256 hash is fixed. A separate fidelity audit of all 251 reviewer comments judged 246 applied, 3 partially applied, and 2 not applied; every affected card carries the verbatim comment and its application result.

\par\section{Release composition}
The final release ships one L1 CSV, one L1/L2/L3 CSV, and three domain L4 CSVs. Public CSVs carry 28 columns: bilingual hierarchy definitions, facet, act-type, the verbatim human-review comment and its application result, and References; full-column originals including scoring and keyword machinery are preserved in the handover package and archive. The validation folder retains the 808-row instruction register, lineage edges, deletion tombstones, merge/decomposition/curation logs, and the audit correction log.
