\par\section*{Abstract}
This report documents four stages of work. First, the second-round human review instructions (an 808-row register with 251 verbatim reviewer comments) were re-applied row by row to the 798 baseline L4 cards, producing a 777-card recovery release. Second, an independent audit pipeline (AC-01 through AC-17) produced a 629-card reviewed master. Third, all 629 rows of the third-round KTSPACE review were read before transformation. The 30 non-empty comments were interpreted and applied as 19 semantic reassignments with definition revision, six deletions, and five same-L3 scope generalisations. Fourth, AC-19 applied two user-approved cross-domain reassignments and one Korean terminology correction after two independent Physical AI audits. No EM or Hybrid EM was re-run at any stage. \textbf{The final release contains 623 L4 cards: General 494, Agentic 66, Physical 63. The 47 substantive L3 categories remain populated, the three Others queues have no assignments, and the 50-row L3 master is byte-identical to the pre-AC-19 master.}
\par\section{Recovery principles}
Valid edits and user-approved merges from the baseline commit were preserved. Only the deletion, merge, split, reassignment, and definition-revision instructions of the second-round review were applied, traced through source\_row\_id lineage. The recovery stage initially preserved the L3 master; AC-14 subsequently added only the explicitly approved G\_SYS\_PERF category and updated the master hash. A separate fidelity audit of all 251 reviewer comments judged 246 applied, 3 partially applied, and 2 not applied; every affected card carries the verbatim comment and its application result.

\par\section{Third-round human review (AC-18)}
The three source tables contained 629 rows: General 487, Agentic 65, and Physical 77. Thirteen General rows and seventeen Physical rows contained a reviewer comment; all Agentic review cells were blank. The 599 uncommented rows were retained byte-for-byte in the full-column master. The 30 commented rows were interpreted against their current card meaning and the unchanged L3 master before any edit was made.
\begin{table}[h]\centering\begin{tabular}{lr}\toprule Round-3 action & Cards\\\midrule Reassign and rewrite & 19\\Delete & 6\\Generalise within the same L3 & 5\\Merge & 0\\Split & 0\\New L4 or L3 & 0\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\caption{Third-round human-review actions}\end{table}
Two independent expert reviewers agreed on the explicit deletions and target categories and separately assessed ambiguous cases. The adjudicator assigned \texttt{G\_INT\_SELF\_006} to \texttt{G\_SYS\_CONTEXT} because this preserved the original contextually inappropriate-content mechanism without inventing manipulative intent. \texttt{P\_INT\_SAFETY\_007} was generalised and retained because no merge had been authorised. Both reviewers agreed that \texttt{P\_INT\_SAFETY\_004} conflicted with Physical Safety and belonged to \texttt{G\_INT\_UNETH}. Physical mechanisms and consequences were retained in every card moved from Physical to General. Old-to-new IDs and six deletion tombstones are recorded in the round-3 decision ledger.
\par\section{Physical AI minimum corrections (AC-19)}
Two independent reviewers audited all 65 Physical AI cards against the frozen L3 master. The user approved only the three highest-confidence minimum corrections. \texttt{P\_SYS\_HARDWARE\_001} was reidentified as \texttt{G\_SYS\_PERF\_017} because sensor and distribution drift cause deployment performance degradation rather than physical breakage or wear. \texttt{P\_INT\_TAMPER\_001} was reidentified as \texttt{G\_SYS\_SECADV\_061} because sensor spoofing and signal injection are adversarial-input attacks covered by Security and Adversarial Robustness Failure and excluded from the physical-tampering boundary. \texttt{P\_SYS\_HARDWARE\_003} retained its ID and hierarchy, while the Korean mistranslation of cosmic ray was corrected from ``우주선'' to ``우주 방사선''. No card was deleted, merged, split, or newly created. The total remained 623, while the domain distribution changed to General 494, Agentic 66, and Physical 63.
\par\section{Release composition}
The final release ships one L1 CSV, one L1/L2/L3 CSV, and three domain L4 CSVs. Public L4 CSVs carry 25 columns: bilingual hierarchy definitions, facet, and act-type. Internal full-column originals retain human-review comments, application results, lineage, references, and historical score fields; no score is exposed on the website. The validation folder retains the 629-row round-3 decision ledger, 30-row application log, AC-19 correction ledger and validation record, methodology notes, old-to-new ID crosswalks, deletion tombstones, earlier review registers, and the audit correction log.
