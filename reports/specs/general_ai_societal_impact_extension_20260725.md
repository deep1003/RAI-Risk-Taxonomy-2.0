# General AI L2 and L3 Extension Specification

Date: 2026-07-25

Source: User-provided draft “RAI Risk Taxonomy v2.0”, dated 2026-07-27.

## Structural change

- Add `Societal Impact (사회적 파급)` as a General AI L2 category.
- Move the nine existing Physical AI `Societal Safety` L3 families under the new General AI L2 with new hierarchy-consistent IDs.
- Preserve the legacy ID mapping and the source provenance of every Physical AI card.
- Keep overlapping or similarly named L3 families. Resolve each L4 assignment by the higher semantic fit.
- Preserve General and Agentic HOLD paths for cards that do not meet the quantitative release criteria.
- Preserve all 1,711 registered L4 IDs and all 1,660 active-card records.

## New L3 families

1. `Energy Consumption and Environmental Pollution (에너지 소비 및 환경 오염)` under General AI Interaction Safety.
2. `Accountability and Governance Gaps (책임성 부족 및 거버넌스 체계 부재)` under General AI Societal Impact.
3. `Lack of Transparency (투명성 부족)` under General AI System Safety.
4. `Fairness (공정성)` under General AI Societal Impact.

## Scope distinctions retained during winner-takes-all assignment

- General accountability covers organizational, institutional, audit, value-chain, incident-response, and remedy mechanisms.
- Agentic accountability remains limited to causal or decision traceability across agents and agent chains.
- Migrated Physical accountability and liability retains its embodied-system and physical-accident scope.
- General transparency covers documentation, disclosure, interpretability, and evaluation evidence.
- Migrated Physical transparency retains embodied-system decisions, deployment trust, and physical interaction.
- General fairness covers systematic group disadvantage, access, opportunity, and social inequality.
- Migrated Physical bias and discrimination retains bias enacted through embodied behavior or physical service.
